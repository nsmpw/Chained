Chained

Share your thoughts [025]

This archive extends the exploration begun in Chained, a publication that used Siamese Connections as metaphors for conditional access. Their chains connect different parts while limiting how far they can move, reflecting the conditions that currently shape the experiences of international students in the United States. The ability to stay, work, and plan for the future is not always a matter of personal choice, raising questions about freedom and the uncertainty of building a life somewhere without knowing how long one can remain.

By presenting selected responses as fragments rather than complete interviews, the archive brings together different personal experiences that share an underlying uncertainty. Together, the publication and archive examine the relationship between attachment and restriction, and how a place can leave a lasting impression regardless of how long one is able to stay.
